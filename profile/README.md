# RFI-IRFOS

### Research Focus Institute · Interdisciplinary Research Facility for Open Sciences

**Frontier ternary logic · open science · ecocentric AI**

---

RFI-IRFOS is an independent Austrian research institute building the **Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS)** — a full-stack, post-binary AI platform (language, compiler, ISA, virtual machine, linear algebra, API, and model) built on balanced-ternary arithmetic {-1, 0, +1}.

Our proof that the model layer works is **albert.** — a ternary Mixture-of-Experts language model trained from scratch that grows its own architecture through autonomous, plateau-gated Net2Net surgery.

### What we build

- **[ternary-intelligence-stack](https://github.com/rfi-irfos/ternary-intelligence-stack)** — the full TIS monorepo (Ternlang language, BET VM, `@sparseskip`, albert.)
- **albert.** — ternary MoE language model · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/rfi-irfos/albert)
- **[All repositories →](https://github.com/orgs/rfi-irfos/repositories)**

Patent pending **A50296/2026** (TIS platform, Austrian Patent Office). Developed and documented for **EU AI Act Art. 13 / 14 / 15** (transparency, human oversight, robustness) compliance by design.

### Legal entity

RFI-IRFOS is a **registered, fully regulated Austrian entity** — not an informal initiative. It is a **not-for-profit**: it earns revenue through multiple streams permitted by its statutes and reinvests at least 90% of surplus into the research mission (at most 10% retained for operations). Surplus is not distributed to members.
